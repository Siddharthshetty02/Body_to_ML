🍕 Simplified Everyday Analogy

"Think of the brain as your memory when shooting free-throws in basketball. After shooting 500 balls, your muscle memory remembers the exact wrist flick needed to score without having to do math each time!"

🧬 What It Does in You (Biology)

Connects billions of tiny brain cells called neurons. Every time you study or practice, the connections get stronger so you remember how to solve problems quickly.

🤖 What It Does in AI (Machine Learning)

Known as Neural Network Weights. The AI stores millions of little dial numbers. When an image enters, it multiplies by those numbers to guess: "That's a cat!"

🚀
Where is this used? Self-driving cars like Tesla use artificial brains to instantly decide when to brake if a ball bounces into the street.
