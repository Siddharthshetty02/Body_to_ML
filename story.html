STEP 1 OF 7 The Sensory Eyes

Capturing Light and Turning it into Numbers

🍕 Simplified Everyday Picture

"Imagine looking at your smartphone screen with a magnifying glass. You'll see that every dog or cat picture is just millions of tiny glowing Red, Green, and Blue light bulbs!"

🧬 What Happens in Your Body

Light bounces off a cute puppy into your pupils. The retinas' rod & cone cells turn the photons into electrical nerve impulses.

🤖 What Happens in the Computer

The computer opens a JPG file and breaks it into a 3D grid of numbers from 0 to 255 for Red, Green, and Blue pixels (called a Tensor).

🚀
Why does this matter? This is how your phone unlocks instantly with FaceID and how Tesla cars detect lane markings!
